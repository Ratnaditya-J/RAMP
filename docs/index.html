RAMP: Robustness Audit for Monitoring Probes

RAMP audits whether a safety signal holds up under honest evaluation. It runs a pre-registered survival ladder (leakage-free cross-fitting, blind labels, distribution shift) over guard models and activation probes, and emits a scoped Signal Validity Card on the robustness axis of safety-signal validity.
